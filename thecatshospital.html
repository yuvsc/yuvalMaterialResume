Our Team

Dedicated to cats
only.

At The Cat's Hospital, we believe your feline companion deserves a veterinary experience tailored entirely to their unique needs — a calm, dog-free environment staffed by professionals who understand feline behavior, physiology, and medicine at a deep level.

We will do everything possible to offer the very best in veterinary care to strengthen the bond you have with your cat, starting when they are a kitten and continuing into their old age.

Dr. Anthony Cosimini, DVM
Founder & Lead Veterinarian

Dr. Cosimini graduated from Iowa State University College of Veterinary Medicine in 1986. After twelve years as an associate veterinarian, he founded The Cat's Hospital in 1999 — bringing over three decades of feline expertise to Wellesley.
